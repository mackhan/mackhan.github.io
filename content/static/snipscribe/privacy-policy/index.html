SnipScribe 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026-10-21

SnipScribe 是 macOS 菜单栏上的截屏识字工具：框选屏幕，识别出的文字直接进剪贴板。它处理的东西都是你屏幕上的内容，所以我们对这些数据只有一条原则：不离开你的 Mac。

1. 信息收集

我们承诺：

  • 不收集任何个人身份信息，不需要注册账号
  • 不收集任何使用数据、统计或崩溃报告
  • 不收集任何设备信息
  • 不收集、不上传你截取的图像和识别出的文字：文字识别全部由系统自带的识别能力在你的 Mac 上完成

2. 数据存储

识别使用的临时图像只存在于内存中，用完即弃，不写入磁盘。使用次数、快捷键、界面语言这类偏好设置保存在你自己的电脑上。应用不设服务器、不做任何云端同步，也没有历史记录功能。

3. 权限说明

应用只申请完成功能所必需的权限：

  • 屏幕录制：你按下快捷键框选屏幕区域时使用，macOS 对截屏类功能统一归入这项权限
  • 辅助功能：仅在你开启「自动粘贴」时需要，用来把识别结果填入当前输入位置
  • 剪贴板：读取你复制的图片用于识别，以及把识别结果写回剪贴板

应用不请求网络、定位、通讯录、相机或麦克风权限。以上权限都可以在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」里随时关闭。

4. 数据共享

SnipScribe 不会与任何第三方共享你的数据，应用内没有任何第三方统计、广告或分析 SDK。

5. 购买与许可

所有识别功能对免费版开放，免费版每天 10 次，0 点自动重置。一次性购买（¥8 / $2.99）只移除每日次数上限，没有订阅、没有广告。购买由 App Store 内购处理，我们不会接触或保存你的支付信息。

6. 用户权利

  • 删除权：删除应用即可彻底移除全部本地数据
  • 控制权：识别只在你主动按下快捷键时发生，你随时可以取消
  • 知情权：你有权了解我们如何处理你的数据

7. 数据安全

因为数据从未离开你的设备，也就没有传输与服务端存储的风险。我们同时遵循平台的安全要求进行本地数据处理。

8. 儿童隐私

我们的应用程序不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不有意收集儿童的个人信息。

9. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。如有重大变更，我们会在应用程序中或在本页面上通知你。

SnipScribe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

SnipScribe is a screenshot-to-text tool that lives in your macOS menu bar: select part of the screen and the recognized text goes straight to your clipboard. Everything it touches comes from your own screen, so we follow one rule only: it never leaves your Mac.

1. Data Collection

We are committed to:

  • Not collecting any personal identification information — no account, no sign-up
  • Not collecting any usage, analytics or crash data
  • Not collecting any device information
  • Not collecting or uploading the images you capture or the text recognized from them — recognition runs entirely on your Mac using the system's built-in capability

2. Data Storage

Captured images exist only in memory while they are being recognized and are discarded afterwards; nothing is written to disk. Preferences such as usage count, hotkeys and interface language are stored on your own computer. The app has no server, performs no cloud sync, and keeps no history.

3. Permissions

The app asks only for what the feature needs:

  • Screen Recording: used when you press the hotkey and select an area — macOS groups all screen capture under this permission
  • Accessibility: needed only if you turn on auto-paste, so the recognized text can be typed into the field you are in
  • Clipboard: reading the image you copied for recognition, and writing the result back

The app does not request network, location, contacts, camera or microphone access. You can revoke any of the above at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

4. Data Sharing

SnipScribe does not share your data with any third party, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

5. Purchases and Licensing

Every recognition feature is available in the free version, limited to 10 uses per day and reset at midnight. A one-time purchase (¥8 / $2.99) only removes that daily limit — no subscription, no ads. Purchases are handled by App Store in-app purchase; we never receive or store your payment information.

6. User Rights

  • Deletion: removing the app removes all local data completely
  • Control: recognition only happens when you press the hotkey, and you can cancel at any time
  • Information: you have the right to know how we handle your data

7. Data Security

Because data never leaves your device, there is no transfer or server-side storage risk. We also follow platform security requirements for local processing.

8. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If there are significant changes, we will notify you in the app or on this page.
