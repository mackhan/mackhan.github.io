KVMini 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026-10-14

KVMini 是跨平台鼠标键盘共享软件，支持 macOS 和 Windows 之间的设备控制和文件传输。我们非常重视您的隐私保护。

1. 信息收集

我们承诺：

  • 不收集任何个人身份信息
  • 不收集任何使用数据或分析数据
  • 不收集任何设备信息
  • 所有数据处理都在您的设备本地完成

2. 数据存储

所有应用程序数据都存储在您的本地设备上，不涉及任何云端数据同步或存储。

3. 数据共享

KVMini 不会与任何第三方共享您的数据。

4. 权限说明

应用程序需要以下权限来提供完整功能：

  • 辅助功能权限：用于监听全局快捷键和模拟鼠标键盘输入
  • 输入监控权限：用于实现跨设备鼠标键盘共享

所有权限都仅在您明确授权后使用，且仅用于应用程序的核心功能。

5. 用户权利

  • 访问权：您可以随时查看应用程序的本地数据
  • 删除权：您可以随时删除应用程序及其所有本地数据
  • 控制权：您可以控制应用程序的权限设置
  • 知情权：您有权了解我们如何处理您的数据

6. 数据安全

我们采取适当的技术措施来保护您的数据安全，遵循平台安全要求。

7. 儿童隐私

我们的应用程序不面向13岁以下的儿童，也不有意收集儿童的个人信息。

8. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。如有重大变更，我们会在应用程序中通知您。

9. 联系方式

联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问或建议，请通过以下方式联系我们：

邮箱：support@kvmini.app

KVMini Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

KVMini is a cross-platform keyboard and mouse sharing tool for macOS and Windows, with clipboard and file transfer between the computers. We take your privacy seriously.

1. Data Collection

We are committed to:

  • Not collecting any personal identification information
  • Not collecting any usage or analytics data
  • Not collecting any device information
  • All data processing is done locally on your device

2. Data Storage

All application data is stored locally on your device. No cloud data synchronization or storage is involved.

3. Data Sharing

KVMini does not share your data with any third party.

4. Permissions

The application requires the following permissions to provide full functionality:

  • 辅助功能权限: 用于监听全局快捷键和模拟鼠标键盘输入
  • 输入监控权限: 用于实现跨设备鼠标键盘共享

All permissions are used only after your explicit authorization and solely for the application's core functionality.

5. User Rights

  • Access: You can view the application's local data at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete the application and all its local data at any time
  • Control: You can control the application's permission settings
  • Information: You have the right to know how we handle your data

6. Data Security

We take appropriate technical measures to protect your data security, following platform security requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If there are significant changes, we will notify you within the application.

9. Contact

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this privacy policy, please contact us:

Email: support@kvmini.app
