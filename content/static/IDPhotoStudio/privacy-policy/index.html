IDPhoto Studio 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026-10-16

IDPhoto Studio 是 macOS 上的证件照制作工具，支持多国规格的抠图、合规校验与打印排版。您的照片是很敏感的个人数据，我们对它的处理只有一条原则：不离开您的电脑。

1. 信息收集

我们承诺：

  • 不收集任何个人身份信息
  • 不收集任何使用数据或分析数据
  • 不收集任何设备信息
  • 不收集、不上传您的照片：抠图、校验、排版全部在您的 Mac 上完成

2. 数据存储

您的照片、处理结果和导出文件都只保存在您自己选择的本地位置。应用不联网、不设服务器、不做任何云端同步。各国证件照规格数据随应用内置，使用时不需要联网查询。

3. 数据共享

IDPhoto Studio 不会与任何第三方共享您的数据，应用内也没有任何第三方统计或广告 SDK。

4. 权限说明

应用只在您主动操作时访问文件：

  • 读取照片：您选择要处理的照片时
  • 写入文件：您导出证件照或打印排版时，保存到您指定的位置

应用不请求网络、定位、通讯录、相机或麦克风权限。

5. 购买与许可

IDPhoto Studio 为一次买断，不订阅、不按次收费。购买流程由应用内购买渠道处理，我们不会接触或保存您的支付信息。

6. 用户权利

  • 删除权：删除应用及其文件即可彻底移除所有数据
  • 控制权：您始终决定照片从哪来、存到哪去
  • 知情权：您有权了解我们如何处理您的数据

7. 数据安全

因为数据从未离开您的设备，也就没有传输与服务端存储的风险。我们同时遵循平台的安全要求进行本地数据处理。

8. 儿童隐私

我们的应用程序不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不有意收集儿童的个人信息。

9. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。如有重大变更，我们会在应用程序中或在本页面上通知您。

10. 联系方式

联系我们

IDPhoto Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

IDPhoto Studio makes ID photos on macOS: background removal, compliance checks and print layout for many country specs. Your photos are sensitive personal data, and we handle them by one rule only: they never leave your computer.

1. Data Collection

We are committed to:

  • Not collecting any personal identification information
  • Not collecting any usage or analytics data
  • Not collecting any device information
  • Not collecting or uploading your photos — cutout, checks and layout all happen on your Mac

2. Data Storage

Your photos, results and exported files are saved only where you choose on your own disk. The app does not connect to the network, has no server, and performs no cloud sync. Country spec data ships inside the app, so no lookup over the network is needed.

3. Data Sharing

IDPhoto Studio does not share your data with any third party, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

4. Permissions

The app touches files only when you ask it to:

  • Reading photos: when you pick a photo to process
  • Writing files: when you export an ID photo or print layout to a folder you choose

The app does not request network, location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

5. Purchases and Licensing

IDPhoto Studio is a one-time purchase — no subscription, no per-use fee. Purchases are handled by the in-app purchase channel; we never receive or store your payment information.

6. User Rights

  • Deletion: removing the app and its files removes all data completely
  • Control: you always decide where photos come from and where they are saved
  • Information: you have the right to know how we handle your data

7. Data Security

Because data never leaves your device, there is no transfer or server-side storage risk. We also follow platform security requirements for local processing.

8. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If there are significant changes, we will notify you in the app or on this page.

10. Contact
