图谱 RokidChart 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026-10-16 · 对应版本 v1.5.1

图谱（RokidChart）是给乐奇 Rokid AI 眼镜用的乐谱图片浏览器：把眼镜里的乐谱图片排成九宫格，镜腿一滑翻页，还能自动滚动、打节拍。它是一个 82KB 的原生 APK，没有任何第三方依赖，也不联网。

一句话说明白：这个应用不联网、不上传、不收集任何数据。你的乐谱图片从头到尾只在眼镜本机读取和显示。

1. 信息收集

我们承诺：

  • 不收集任何个人身份信息
  • 不收集任何使用数据或分析数据
  • 不收集任何设备信息或标识符
  • 不读取、不上传你的乐谱图片 —— 图片只在眼镜本机解码并显示

2. 数据存储

应用不保存任何自己的数据副本，只记录你看到第几页这类界面状态。乐谱图片始终留在你的眼镜存储里，应用不会复制、移动或改动它们。

3. 数据共享

图谱不会与任何第三方共享你的数据。APK 内不含任何第三方统计、广告或崩溃上报 SDK —— 这是它能做到 82KB 的原因之一。

4. 权限说明

应用只申请下面这些权限，全部和「读到你的乐谱图片」有关：

  • 读取图片（READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED）：Android 13 及以上，读取你选择的图片
  • 读取外部存储（READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE）：仅 Android 12 及以下需要
  • 管理外部存储（MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE）：眼镜上的乐谱文件分散在 Download / Pictures / Documents / DCIM / Movies / Rokid 多个公共目录，需要这个权限才能一次把它们都扫出来。它只用于读取，不会删除或修改任何文件
  • 保持唤醒（WAKE_LOCK）：看谱或提词时维持屏幕常亮，避免演奏到一半息屏

应用不申请网络（INTERNET）权限，因此在技术上无法把任何数据发出去。它也不申请定位、通讯录、相机或麦克风权限。

5. 用户权利

  • 控制权：随时在系统设置里撤掉存储权限，应用会退回空列表而不是崩溃
  • 删除权：卸载应用即彻底移除，不留任何数据
  • 知情权：你有权了解我们如何处理你的数据

6. 数据安全

因为数据从未离开设备、应用也不具备联网能力，不存在传输或服务端存储的风险。

7. 儿童隐私

我们的应用程序不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不有意收集儿童的个人信息。

8. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。如有重大变更，我们会在本页面上通知你。

9. 联系方式

联系我们

Tupu (RokidChart) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16 · for v1.5.1

Tupu (RokidChart) is a sheet-music image viewer for Rokid AI glasses: it lays out your sheet-music images in a grid, you turn pages by swiping the temple, and it can auto-scroll and keep a beat. It is an 82 KB native APK with no third-party dependencies and no network access.

In one line: this app does not connect to the network, does not upload anything, and collects no data. Your sheet music is read and displayed on the glasses, and nowhere else.

1. Data Collection

We are committed to:

  • Not collecting any personal identification information
  • Not collecting any usage or analytics data
  • Not collecting any device information or identifiers
  • Not reading or uploading your sheet-music images — they are decoded and shown on the device only

2. Data Storage

The app keeps no copy of your data; it only remembers interface state such as which page you are on. Your images stay in the glasses' storage — the app never copies, moves or modifies them.

3. Data Sharing

Tupu does not share your data with any third party. The APK contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK — part of the reason it fits in 82 KB.

4. Permissions

The app requests only the following permissions, all related to reading your sheet music:

  • Read images (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED): on Android 13 and above, to read the images you select
  • Read external storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): needed only on Android 12 and below
  • Manage external storage (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): on the glasses, sheet music is scattered across several public folders — Download / Pictures / Documents / DCIM / Movies / Rokid. This permission lets the app find them all in one pass. It is used for reading only; nothing is deleted or modified
  • Keep awake (WAKE_LOCK): keeps the screen on while you read or are prompted, so it does not dim mid-performance

The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send any data out. It also does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

5. User Rights

  • Control: revoke the storage permission in system settings at any time; the app falls back to an empty list rather than crashing
  • Deletion: uninstalling the app removes everything, leaving no data behind
  • Information: you have the right to know how we handle your data

6. Data Security

Because data never leaves the device and the app has no network capability, there is no transfer or server-side storage risk.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If there are significant changes, we will notify you on this page.

9. Contact

Contact us
