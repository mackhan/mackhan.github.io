生命树 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026-10-16

生命树（LifeTree）是一款健康习惯 App：用四维健康树格（睡眠 / 饮食 / 运动 / 压力）测出你的树型，每天打卡让生命树生长，并给出寿命估算与改善建议。健康数据比照片更私密，所以我们的做法很直接：它只留在你的手机上，哪里也不去。

1. 信息收集

你填写的这些内容只会保存在本机：

  • 基本信息：年龄、性别、地区
  • 烟酒情况与基因信息（APOE / FOXO3 / ALDH2，可跳过）
  • 16 题树格问卷的答案
  • 每天的打卡记录与营养包流水

我们承诺：不收集任何个人身份信息、使用数据、分析数据或设备标识符；不联网、不上传、不设账号、不需要注册。

2. 数据存储

所有数据都用 iOS 本地数据库存在你的设备上，不开启任何云端同步，也没有服务器保存副本。删除 App 即彻底移除全部数据；你也可以在「我的 → 清除所有数据」里随时清空。

3. 数据共享

生命树不会与任何第三方共享你的数据，App 内没有任何第三方统计或广告 SDK。

4. 权限说明

App 不请求网络、定位、通讯录、相机、麦克风或健康数据（HealthKit）权限。你填写的内容全部由自己手动输入，App 不会去读取系统的健康记录。

5. 关于社区内容

社区里的动态、点赞与举报都在本地生成与审核，不会上传，也不存在其他用户能看到的真实服务器内容。

6. 重要声明

寿命预测是基于群体研究的统计估算，不是医疗诊断，也不能替代专业医疗建议。请勿据此做出任何医疗决定；如有健康问题，请咨询专业医疗机构。App 内提供心理支持热线入口，供你需要时使用。

7. 用户权利

  • 访问权：随时在 App 内查看自己的档案与全部记录
  • 删除权：随时清除所有数据，或卸载 App 彻底移除
  • 控制权：基因等敏感项可以跳过，不填也能正常使用
  • 知情权：你有权了解我们如何处理你的数据

8. 数据安全

数据不出设备，因此不存在传输与服务端存储的风险。App 同时遵循 iOS 平台的数据保护要求。

9. 儿童隐私

我们的应用程序不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不有意收集儿童的个人信息。

10. 政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。如有重大变更，我们会在应用程序中或在本页面上通知你。

LifeTree Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

LifeTree is a health habit app: a four-dimension Health ArborType (sleep / diet / activity / stress) places you in one of 16 tree types, daily check-ins grow your life tree, and you get a life expectancy estimate with suggestions. Health data is more private than photos, so our approach is simple: it stays on your phone and goes nowhere else.

1. Data Collection

What you enter is stored only on your device:

  • Basics: age, sex, region
  • Smoking, drinking and genetic information (APOE / FOXO3 / ALDH2 — optional, you can skip it)
  • Your answers to the 16-question ArborType survey
  • Daily check-ins and nutrient pack history

We do not collect any personal identification information, usage data, analytics data or device identifiers. There is no network access, no upload, no account and no sign-up.

2. Data Storage

All data lives in the on-device iOS database. No cloud sync is enabled and no server keeps a copy. Deleting the app removes everything; you can also erase all data at any time under Profile → Erase All Data.

3. Data Sharing

LifeTree does not share your data with any third party, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

4. Permissions

The app does not request network, location, contacts, camera, microphone or HealthKit access. Everything is entered by you manually; the app never reads your system health records.

5. Community Content

Posts, likes and reports in the community are generated and reviewed on-device. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no real server content visible to other users.

6. Important Notice

The life expectancy estimate is a statistical estimate based on population studies. It is not a medical diagnosis and does not replace professional medical advice. Please do not make medical decisions based on it; consult a medical professional for any health concern. The app also provides a mental health hotline entry point if you need it.

7. User Rights

  • Access: view your profile and all records in the app at any time
  • Deletion: erase all data at any time, or uninstall the app to remove everything
  • Control: sensitive items such as genetics can be skipped — the app works fine without them
  • Information: you have the right to know how we handle your data

8. Data Security

Because data never leaves the device, there is no transfer or server-side storage risk. The app also follows iOS platform data protection requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If there are significant changes, we will notify you in the app or on this page.
