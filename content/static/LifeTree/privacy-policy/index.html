生命树 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026-10-28（适用版本 1.15.0）

生命树（LifeTree）是一款健康习惯 App：用 50 道题测出你的四维健康树格（睡眠 / 饮食 / 运动 / 压力），每天打卡让生命树生长。健康数据比照片更私密，所以我们的做法很直接：健康数据只留在你的手机上，不上传，也不进入 iCloud 同步。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

你填写或授权的内容默认只保存在你的设备上：

  • 50 题问卷的答案，以及由此算出的四维健康度与树型
  • 每天的打卡记录、饮食照片、营养包流水、挑战进度
  • 你自己写的文字记录
  • 建档时填写的生日，以及可选的健康档案：性别、地区、烟酒情况、基因信息（APOE / FOXO3 / ALDH2 等，可全部跳过）
  • 生日只用于在设备上计算「已过去的时间」和「剩余营养包」的倒计时（都按秒算，每天减少一天的量，86,400），不改变树的总营养包，也不上传、不进 iCloud 同步。建档时必填，之后不可修改（需要改动请删除档案重建）

我们不设账号、不需要注册，不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、通讯录、定位或设备标识符，也没有第三方统计或广告 SDK（我们自己写的那份匿名统计见第 5.2 节）。

2. 「健康」App（HealthKit）数据

只有你主动授权后，App 才会读取「健康」App 中的数据用于自动填写打卡，包括：睡眠时长、睡眠分段、步数、运动时间、爬楼、静息心率、心率变异性、正念时长、饮食能量与蛋白质。你也可以在打卡时把运动与正念记录写回「健康」App。

这些数据只在本机参与计算，不会被上传、不会写入 iCloud，也不会用于任何其他目的。你可以在 iOS 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 健康 中随时撤销授权。

2.1 麦克风与语音识别

  • 麦克风 / 语音识别：只有你在打卡页点右上角麦克风、并按下录音按钮时才会调用。识别使用 iOS 系统自带的设备端识别（不经过任何服务器），录音与转出的文字都不会离开你的手机，也不保存、不上传、不进入 iCloud。
  • 转出的文字只在手机上做本地解析，用来预填打卡表单；必须你自己核对并点提交才会被记录，我们不会替你写入任何内容。设备不支持端上识别时会直接提示并停止，不会改用云端识别。

2.2 相机、照片与「运动与健身」

  • 相机 / 相册：只有你在饮食打卡里主动点「拍照」或「相册」时才会调用。照片写入 App 自己的目录，只留在本机，不上传、不进入 iCloud 同步，也不进入 iCloud 备份。
  • 照片识别（可选）：照片的识别同样在本机完成，只作为候选供你挑选，份量由你自己确定；不会自动记入打卡。未内置识别模型时该入口不会出现。
  • 运动与健身（CMPedometer）：只有你打开「智能久坐」后才会读取手机计步器，用来判断当前这一小时是否走动过——走动过就跳过这次久坐提醒。步数即读即用，不保存、不上传。你可以随时在 iOS 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 运动与健身 中关闭，关闭后久坐提醒照常按时间发出。

3. 数据存储与 iCloud 同步

数据默认用 iOS 本地数据库存在你的设备上。你可以在「我的 → iCloud」中开启同步，此时只有以下四类会同步：

  • 昵称
  • 营养包记录
  • 挑战进度
  • 你写的文字记录
  • 你加入了哪些习惯小组、哪天点过「做到了」

打卡记录、问卷答案、健康档案以及「健康」App 的数据一律不同步。同步走的是你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库，开发者无法查看。若你的 Apple ID 属于中国内地，相关数据由「云上贵州」（GCBD）运营并存储在境内。

关闭同步开关或退出 iCloud 登录后，新的同步立即停止。

3.1 小组里的匿名状态（1.7.0 起，默认开启、可随时关闭）

习惯小组是 App 预设好的固定目录，你不能自建、不能发帖、不能写字、不能发图，也没有点赞、评论、关注或私信。加入小组并打开「向小组公开我的匿名进度」后，App 会把三个你自己点出来的数字写入苹果 CloudKit 的公有数据库：

  • 今天有没有点「做到了」
  • 连续坚持了多少天
  • 加入这个小组多少天

同组的人能看到一个随机代号（如 a3f1）加这三个数字。没有昵称、头像、地区、生日，也没有任何来自「健康」的数据——步数、睡眠、心率这些一个都不上传（见第 2 节）。因为不能写字也不能发图，这里不存在可创作的内容。

只能说是「匿名」，不能说是「认不出是谁」：CloudKit 公有库的每条记录都会携带写入者的 iCloud 账号标识，理论上可以把同一个人的多条记录归并到一起。我们的处理是：代号每 7 天轮换一次、只读取最近 3 天的记录、不保存也不反查任何身份信息。如果你不接受这一点，请不要打开这个开关。

数据来源只有你自己的点击。你随时可以撤回：

  • 在小组详情页关掉「向小组公开我的匿名进度」——立即停止上传，并删除你已经上传的那条
  • 退出小组——同样会删除云上那条
  • 「我的 → 清除所有数据」——本机数据与云上那条一并清除

看到不对的代号，点它右边的 ⋯ 可以举报（只从几个预设理由里选，不能自由输入）或选择「不看 TA」（只记在你本机）。举报会写入一张只有开发者能读的表，我们会据此处理。

4. 小组件、控制中心、灵动岛与锁屏实时活动

主屏 / 锁屏小组件与控制中心的「快速打卡」按钮只显示「今天打了几张卡」这类计数，不含任何健康数值。

若你开启「灵动岛与锁屏进度」，锁屏和灵动岛会显示今天的打卡项、你今天喝了多少毫升水与下次提醒时间，并可直接「+1 杯」。这些内容通过 App 组（App Group）在你的设备上本地读写，不联网，也可在「我的 → 提醒」中随时关闭。

5. 提醒与通知

打卡提醒由你的设备本地发出（iOS 26 及以上使用系统闹钟，更早版本使用本地通知），提醒的设置项、铃声与推送语都保存在本机，不会上传。

5.1 久坐与喝水提醒、喝水记录

  • 久坐 / 喝水提醒同样是本机本地通知。通知上的「+1 杯」「+1 瓶」「已起身」「15 分钟后提醒」按钮只在你的设备上改本地记录，不联网。
  • 喝水与起身记录（每天喝了多少毫升、点了几次「已起身」）保存在 App 组里，只留在本机，不上传、不进入 iCloud 同步、也不会写入「健康」App。最多保留约 400 天，用于生成周报 / 月报 / 年报。
  • 专注模式：你可以为某个专注模式添加「生命树」过滤条件来暂停久坐 / 喝水提醒。App 只知道「是否被暂停」，读不到你开的是哪个专注模式，这个状态也只存在本机。

5.2 匿名使用统计（第一方，只在本机）

为了知道「新用户第一次打开走到哪一步就流失了」，我们自己写了一段极简的统计代码，不接入任何第三方 SDK。它只记这几种事件：首次安装、每次启动、是否高级版、付费墙从哪儿弹出、购买结果、报告页打开、打了哪个维度的卡（只记维度名，不记分数、不记任何健康数值）。

  • 事件里有一个本机随机生成的匿名安装 id，不含姓名、邮箱、手机号、设备序列号或广告标识符（IDFA）；重装 App 会换一个新 id，所以无法把两次安装连成同一个人。
  • 当前版本没有配置任何上传通道：这些事件只写进本机的一个文件里，一个字节都不会发出去。将来若要开启上传，只会上传同样这几个字段，并且会先在本机征得你的同意。
  • 你可以随时在「我的 → 支持 → 诊断」里关掉它；关掉后连本机文件都不再写入。
  • 本 App 不读 IDFA，也不弹「允许追踪」提示——因为这里确实没有可追踪的东西。

5.3 本机诊断日志（1.15.0 起）

为了在崩溃或保存失败时能定位原因，App 会在本机保留一份滚动日志（最近 1000 条、7 天自动清理），记录级别、分类与错误码（只写 domain#code 这样的错误码，不记录文件路径、不记录账号）。

  • 日志不含任何健康数据，不上传，也不进入 iCloud 备份。
  • 你可以在「我的 → 支持 → 诊断」里查看、搜索、复制、导出为文件或一键清空；导出时你先看到的就是全部内容，没有隐藏字段。

6. 联网说明

  • iCloud 同步：仅在你开启后，与苹果服务器同步上面第 3 节列出的数据。
  • 小组匿名状态：仅在你打开「向小组公开我的匿名进度」后，向苹果 CloudKit 公有数据库写入上面第 3.1 节列出的三个数字。这是全 App 唯一一处会把数据交给同组其他人看到的地方，除此之外没有任何上传。
  • 检查更新：只在你点击时，向 Apple 的公开接口发送一次本 App 的标识，用于查询是否有新版本。
  • 购买：若你购买高级版，交易完全由 Apple 处理，我们收不到你的支付信息。
  • 科研进展的内容更新：打开「科研进展」时，App 会向苹果 CloudKit 公有数据库请求一次文章列表，用于在不发版的情况下补充新研究。这个请求只下载内容，不上传任何数据：不含你的账号、设备标识或任何健康信息。请求失败时静默使用上次内容或 App 内置文章，不会弹错、不会变空白。

除此之外，App 不主动联网。除上面第 3.1 节的三个数字外，App 不向任何服务器上传数据（第 5.2 节的匿名统计当前也只写本机）。

7. 数据共享

生命树不会向任何第三方出售、出租或共享你的数据。App 内没有第三方统计、广告或社交 SDK；第 5.2 节那份统计是我们自己写的，只落本机，当前不上传，也就不存在交给任何第三方。

8. 重要声明

App 中的「树的营养包」是一棵虚拟树的游戏化数值，单位是秒（1 营养包 = 1 秒），只由公开人群生命表基准（男 76.3 / 女 81.8 岁，随年龄衰减）、四维健康度加成和你打卡获得的附加营养包决定；树种只用于外观画像，不参与计算，地区 / 基因 / 烟酒同样不参与。它不预测、也不涉及任何人的寿命，App 界面里也不出现「寿命」二字。四维健康建议参考公开的群体研究，存在不确定性，不是医疗诊断或治疗建议，不能替代专业医疗意见。如有健康问题，请咨询医生。App 内提供心理支持信息，供你需要时使用。

9. 你的权利

  • 查看：随时在 App 内查看自己的档案与全部记录
  • 删除：在「我的 → 清除所有数据」中清空本机数据；卸载 App 会连同所有本机数据一起移除
  • 删除已同步内容：关闭同步并清除数据后，已同步到 iCloud 私有库的内容也会在同步时移除
  • 撤回小组公开：随时关掉「向小组公开我的匿名进度」，已上传的那条会被删除
  • 选择权：基因等敏感项可以跳过；「健康」App 授权可以拒绝，App 仍可手动打卡

10. 数据安全

健康数据不出设备；同步数据经你的 iCloud 账户加密传输与存储，并遵循 iOS 平台的数据保护要求。

11. 儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不有意收集儿童的个人信息。

12. 政策变更

我们可能不时更新本隐私政策。如有重大变更，会在 App 内或本页面通知你。

LifeTree Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28 (applies to version 1.15.0)

LifeTree is a health habit app: a 50-question survey places you in a four-dimension Health ArborType (sleep / diet / activity / stress), and daily check-ins grow your life tree. Health data is more private than photos, so our approach is simple: it stays on your phone, is never uploaded, and never syncs through iCloud.

1. What we collect

What you enter or authorize is stored on your device by default:

  • Your answers to the 50-question survey, plus the four-dimension health scores and tree type derived from them
  • Daily check-ins, meal photos, nutrition-pack history and challenge progress
  • Notes you write yourself
  • Your birthday, set when you create your profile, plus optional profile items: sex, region, smoking and drinking habits, genetic information (APOE / FOXO3 / ALDH2 and similar — all skippable)
  • Your birthday is used on device only for the "time elapsed" and remaining-pack countdown (both in seconds, one day's worth — 86,400 — less each day). It never changes the tree's total packs, is never uploaded and never syncs through iCloud. It is required when you create your profile and cannot be edited afterwards (delete the profile and start over if you need to change it)

There is no account and no sign-up. We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, contacts, location or device identifiers, and there is no third-party analytics or advertising SDK (for the anonymous statistics we wrote ourselves, see section 5.2).

2. Apple Health (HealthKit) data

Only after you grant permission does the app read Health data to pre-fill check-ins: sleep duration and stages, steps, exercise time, flights climbed, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, mindful minutes, and dietary energy and protein. You may also write your exercise and mindfulness entries back to Health.

This data is used on-device only — never uploaded, never written to iCloud, and never used for any other purpose. Revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

2.1 Microphone and speech recognition

  • Microphone / speech recognition: used only when you tap the microphone in Check-in and press record. Recognition uses iOS on-device recognition (no server is involved): the audio and the resulting text never leave your phone, are never stored, never uploaded and never synced through iCloud.
  • The text is parsed on-device only, to pre-fill the check-in form. Nothing is saved until you review it and tap submit — the app never writes entries on your behalf. If the device does not support on-device recognition it stops and says so; it never falls back to cloud recognition.

2.2 Camera, photos and Motion & Fitness

  • Camera / photo library: used only when you tap "Camera" or "Library" in a meal check-in. Photos are written to the app's own folder and stay on this device — never uploaded, never synced through iCloud, never included in iCloud backups.
  • Photo recognition (optional): any recognition also runs on this device and only suggests candidates for you to pick from; you set the portion yourself and nothing is logged automatically. When no recognition model is bundled, the entry does not appear.
  • Motion & Fitness (CMPedometer): read only after you turn on the smart move reminder, to tell whether you've walked during the current hour — if you have, that move reminder is skipped. Steps are used on the spot and never stored or uploaded. You can turn it off at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness; reminders then fire on schedule as usual.

3. Storage and iCloud sync

Data is kept in the on-device iOS database by default. You can turn on sync under Profile → iCloud; when you do, only these kinds sync:

  • Nickname
  • Nutrition pack records
  • Challenge progress
  • Notes you write
  • Which habit groups you joined and which days you tapped "done"

Check-ins, survey answers, your profile and all Health data never sync. Syncing uses your own private iCloud database that the developer cannot see. If your Apple ID is registered in mainland China, that data is operated by Guizhou-Cloud Big Data (GCBD) and stored in mainland China.

Turning the switch off or signing out of iCloud stops further syncing immediately.

3.1 Anonymous group progress (since 1.7.0, on by default, off whenever you like)

Habit groups are a fixed catalogue built into the app. You cannot create your own, cannot post text, cannot upload images, and there are no likes, comments, follows or direct messages. Once you join a group and turn on "Share my anonymous progress with groups", the app writes three numbers, all produced by your own taps, to Apple's public CloudKit database:

  • Whether you tapped "I did it" today
  • How many days your streak has run
  • How many days you've been in the group

Others in the same group see a random code (like a3f1) plus those three numbers. There is no nickname, photo, region or birthday, and nothing at all from Apple Health — steps, sleep and heart rate are never uploaded (see section 2). Because no text and no images can be posted, there is no user-created content here.

This is anonymous, not untraceable. Every record in a public CloudKit database carries the iCloud account identifier of whoever wrote it, so in principle several records can be linked back to one person. What we do about it: the code rotates every 7 days, the app only reads the last 3 days of records, and we neither store nor look up any identity information. If that isn't acceptable to you, please leave the switch off.

The only input is your own tap, and you can withdraw at any time:

  • Turn off "Share my anonymous progress with groups" on the group page — uploading stops immediately and your existing entry is deleted
  • Leave the group — your entry is deleted as well
  • Use Me → Erase all data — on-device data and your cloud entry are both removed

If a code looks wrong, tap the ⋯ beside it to report it (you pick from a few preset reasons; there is no free-text field) or to hide it (stored on your device only). Reports go into a table only the developer can read, and we act on them.

4. Widgets, Control Center, Dynamic Island and Live Activity

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and the Control Center "Quick check-in" button show only counts such as "check-ins today" and no health values.

If you turn on "Dynamic Island & Lock Screen", the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show today's check-in items, how many millilitres of water you've had today and the next reminder time, with a "+1 glass" button. Everything is read and written locally through an App Group, never over the network, and can be turned off under Profile → Reminders.

5. Reminders and notifications

Check-in reminders are delivered locally by your device (system alarms on iOS 26 and later, local notifications on earlier versions). Your reminder settings, sounds and messages stay on-device and are never uploaded.

5.1 Move and water reminders, water log

  • Move / water reminders are local notifications too. The "+1 glass", "+1 bottle", "I'm up" and "remind me in 15 minutes" buttons on a notification only change local records on your device; they make no network requests.
  • Water and stand-up logs (how many millilitres a day, how many times you tapped "I'm up") are stored in an App Group and stay on this device — never uploaded, never synced through iCloud, never written to Health. They are kept for about 400 days to build weekly, monthly and yearly reports.
  • Focus: you can add a LifeTree filter to a Focus to pause move / water reminders. The app only knows whether they're paused — it cannot see which Focus you turned on, and that flag is stored on-device only.

5.2 Anonymous usage statistics (first-party, on-device)

To understand where new users drop off during their first run, we wrote a minimal statistics module ourselves — no third-party SDK is involved. It records only these events: first install, every app start, whether you're on Pro, where the paywall was opened from, purchase result, report opened, and which habit dimension you checked in (the dimension name only — never the score and never any health value).

  • Each event carries a randomly generated anonymous install id. It contains no name, email, phone number, device serial or advertising identifier (IDFA). Reinstalling the app generates a new id, so two installs can never be linked to the same person.
  • In this version no upload channel is configured: the events are written to a file on your device and not a single byte is sent anywhere. If uploading is ever enabled, it would carry exactly these same fields, and only after asking you on-device first.
  • You can turn it off at any time under Profile → Support → Diagnostics; once off, nothing is even written to the local file.
  • The app does not read IDFA and shows no tracking prompt, because there is genuinely nothing to track.

5.3 On-device diagnostics log (since 1.15.0)

So a crash or a failed save can actually be diagnosed, the app keeps a rolling log on your device (last 1000 entries, cleared after 7 days) recording level, category and an error code (only in the form domain#code — no file paths, no account names).

  • The log contains no health data, is never uploaded, and is excluded from iCloud backup.
  • You can browse it, search it, copy it, export it to a file, or clear it under Profile → Support → Diagnostics. What you see when you export is the whole thing — there are no hidden fields.

6. Network activity

  • iCloud sync: only if you enable it, syncing the data listed in section 3 with Apple's servers.
  • Anonymous group progress: only if you turn on "Share my anonymous progress with groups", writing the three numbers listed in section 3.1 to Apple's public CloudKit database. This is the only place in the app where anything you enter becomes visible to other people; apart from it there is no upload at all.
  • Check for updates: only when you tap it, sending the app identifier once to Apple's public API to look for a new version.
  • Purchases: if you buy the Pro version, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple; we receive no payment details.
  • Research article updates: when you open Research, the app requests the article list once from Apple's public CloudKit database, so new studies can appear without an app update. This request only downloads content and uploads nothing — no account, device identifier or health information is sent. If the request fails, the app silently keeps the previous copy or the bundled articles: no error dialog, no blank screen.

Apart from these, the app does not connect to the network on its own. Except for the three numbers in section 3.1, the app uploads nothing to any server (the anonymous statistics in section 5.2 are written on-device only for now).

7. Sharing

LifeTree never sells, rents or shares your data with any third party. There are no third-party analytics, advertising or social SDKs in the app. The statistics in section 5.2 are our own code, stored on-device and not uploaded in this version, so nothing reaches any third party.

8. Important notice

The "nutrition packs" are a game value for a virtual tree, measured in seconds (1 pack = 1 second). They are set by a population-level baseline taken from published life-table statistics (76.3 years for male / 81.8 for female, decaying with age), plus a bonus from your four-dimension health and the extra packs you earn. The tree species is visual only and is not an input; region, genetics and smoking / drinking are not inputs either. They do not predict or involve any person's lifespan, and the word "lifespan" does not appear in the app's interface. The four-dimension advice draws on published population research, is uncertain, and is not a medical diagnosis or treatment advice; it does not replace professional medical advice. Please consult a doctor for any health concern. The app also provides mental health support information if you need it.

9. Your rights

  • Access: view your profile and all records in the app at any time
  • Deletion: erase all data under Profile → Erase all data; uninstalling the app removes all on-device data with it
  • Deleting synced content: after turning sync off and erasing data, content already in your private iCloud database is removed on sync
  • Withdrawing group sharing: turn off "Share my anonymous progress with groups" at any time and the entry you uploaded is deleted
  • Choice: sensitive items such as genetics can be skipped; you can deny Health access and still log everything manually

10. Data security

Health data never leaves the device. Synced data is transmitted and stored encrypted under your iCloud account, following iOS platform data protection requirements.
